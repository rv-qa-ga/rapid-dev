h1. Test Management (TM) – Space home

This space is the *central place for test management*: strategy, environments, automation, planning, system knowledge, and templates. Structure follows a *QA Guild–style* pillar layout so you can find the right artifact quickly.

*All primary content in this space lives under TM. Lloyd’s program documentation remains here.*
